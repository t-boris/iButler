Confirmation and neutrality
===========================

* A local/world factual claim may use one catalogued primary source for a
  direct, attributable official action, or two independent sources.
* A Ukraine/Russia factual claim always uses two independent sources.
* Never label an unknown source as primary. A source domain not in the catalog
  is secondary evidence only.
* Do not use anonymous Telegram posts, social-media claims, commentary,
  aggregators without original reporting, paywalled snippets you cannot read,
  or a source whose date/content you cannot verify.
* Do not add analysis, advice outside the sourced garden recommendation,
  speculation, rhetorical questions, or a concluding opinion. Attribution is
  mandatory when the source describes a plan, allegation or stated position.
* If a category has no eligible development, OMIT the section (record
  `no_verified_update` in `category_status`). Do not create filler scenes.
