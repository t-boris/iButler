## Context

After release 0.11.0 the owner again encountered “Материалы разговора скрыты: владелец не подтверждён.” DEC-004, REQ-005 and the earlier Q-007 answer imposed that Display gate. These records and DEC-008 were checked. The owner now explicitly rejects this behavior: “не нужна мне эта проверка если не работает — я 99% один и никто не видит переписку и так”. This is a new decision overriding the earlier answer; do not ask it again.

## Decision

The local written conversation, Display cards and Display history must remain visible without camera/voice owner verification and without observation permission. Selecting cards, clicking their local controls and clearing Display history also require no biometric check. Keep persistent history until explicit Clear. A failed state poll keeps already rendered conversation material visible while reporting service connectivity separately.

Remove the Display-specific private placeholder from the native panel. Recordings/observation/gallery access, capture consent and the existing validation/approval rules for actual operations keep their own policies; Display visibility must not inherit them.

This supersedes the Display visibility/action portion of DEC-004, DEC-008/Q-007 and REQ-005/REQ-011. The persistent-history decision remains in effect.

## Verification

Automated tests must cover Display reads, selection, controls and Clear with both verification gates denied, persistence across restart and failed UI polls. Keep the independent Recordings denial tests. Release through a new branch, automated test/build and CI gates, then normal deployment.
