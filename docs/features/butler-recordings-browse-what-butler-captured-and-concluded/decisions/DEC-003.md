## Context

The selected 30-day audio retention policy applies to marked meetings.

## Alternatives

1. Automatically mark every detected meeting for audio retention

## Decision

The owner manually marks a current detected meeting to retain its microphone and system audio.

## Reason

The owner selected explicit marking for each meeting.

## Consequences

Detected meetings are not automatically marked for audio retention.

## Implementation

The current detected call can be explicitly marked in Recordings; its mark starts at the owner action and is not inherited by another call or restart.
