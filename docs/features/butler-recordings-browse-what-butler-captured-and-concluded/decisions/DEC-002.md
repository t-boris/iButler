## Context

DEC-001 establishes fixed local transcript retention but does not specify its duration.

## Alternatives

1. Retain transcripts for 90 days
2. Choose another fixed retention period

## Decision

Retain all in-scope transcripts locally for 30 days.

## Reason

The owner selected the same retention period as for marked-meeting audio.

## Consequences

Locally retained transcripts expire after 30 days.
