## Context

A timeline item can contain media, facts, transcripts, and other records with different retention rules. The existing requirement does not specify the scope of preserve and delete controls or their effect on fixed retention periods.

## Alternatives

1. Preserve all associated content indefinitely, and delete the entire timeline item including facts, transcripts, and media. This would require exceptions to the existing 30-day retention decisions.
2. Delete the entire timeline item and its associated content; preserve the item while retaining the fixed 30-day expiry for transcripts and marked-meeting audio.

## Decision

Preserve and delete controls apply to the selected media file. Facts, transcripts, and other associated records follow their own retention policies.

## Reason

The team selected media-level controls, preserving the existing independent retention rules for associated records.

## Consequences

REQ-004 must define controls for individual media files and specify that deletion applies only to the selected file. Facts, transcripts, and other associated records remain subject to their own retention policies. REQ-007 and the 30-day retention decision for marked-meeting audio remain in force.
