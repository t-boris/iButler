## Context

The feature scope excluded full meeting originals pending an owner decision, while Q-002 selected 30-day retention of microphone and system audio for marked meetings and excluded camera video.

## Alternatives

1. Retain microphone and system audio from marked meetings for 30 days; defer retention of meeting video and other originals.
2. Exclude retention of all meeting originals, including audio from marked meetings.

## Decision

Retain audio and video originals of manually marked detected meetings locally for 30 days.

## Reason

The team selected audio and video retention to resolve the scope contradiction and include both media types for marked meetings.

## Consequences

Update the feature scope and requirements to include 30-day retention of marked-meeting audio and video. This supersedes the video exclusion in Q-002. Define applicable capture and participant-consent gates, marking behavior, retention and deletion criteria before implementation.
