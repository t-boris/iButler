## Context

On 2026-10-28 Boris reports owner verification falsely blocks him and says he is always alone in the room; he explicitly requests disabling mandatory automatic owner confirmation for local use.

## Alternatives

1. Continue requiring automatic owner confirmation for local history
2. Treat the owner as biometrically verified even without evidence

## Decision

Implement explicit owner-controlled single-user mode that disables mandatory automatic owner-identity gating for local use without reporting a forged verified identity. Stored recordings and observations should remain browsable during transient identity or action-permission failure. Preserve manual pause and stop, explicit recording off, revoked consent and existing participant permissions; raw media and playback remain under stricter gates with generation checks, and no new external transmission permission is granted.

## Reason

The owner reports that automatic owner verification falsely blocks his own use and explicitly chooses this mode.

## Consequences

Supersedes the mandatory owner-verification portion of DEC-004 and DEC-009's retained Recordings policy when the owner enables single-user mode; identity claims and participant permissions remain distinct. Implemented and installed with synthetic regression coverage; owner verification of real history remains open.
