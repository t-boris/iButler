## Statement

The system shall show the stored facts and any retained transcript for a selected timeline item, together with its source and time.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Selecting an observation shows its stored facts, including their kind and available detail, topic, activity, person or identity, confidence, and basis.
- [ ] The detail identifies the observation's source and time.
- [ ] When a transcript exists for the selected item, it is available in the detail.
