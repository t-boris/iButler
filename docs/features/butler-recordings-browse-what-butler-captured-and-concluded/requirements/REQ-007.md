## Statement

The system shall retain local transcripts of the owner's speech and detected calls for 30 days, subject to existing capture and consent controls.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The owner's speech and detected calls are transcribed and retained locally, subject to existing capture and consent controls.
- [x] Each retained transcript expires after 30 days.

## Implementation evidence

Implemented in `butler_ambient/recordings.py`, `store.py`, capture/listening hooks and the native Recordings pane. Automated regressions: `implementation/ambient-awareness/tests/test_recordings.py`; full release verification remains in progress.
