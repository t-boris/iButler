## Statement

The system shall provide a Recordings section in the Butler panel with a day-grouped, source-filterable timeline of expandable activity or call sessions and their observations.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A Recordings section is accessible from the Butler panel.
- [ ] The timeline is grouped by day and can be filtered by capture source.
- [ ] Consecutive observations with the same activity or call appear as expandable sessions; their individual observations remain accessible.
- [ ] Timeline entries expose their time range and available-content indicators.
