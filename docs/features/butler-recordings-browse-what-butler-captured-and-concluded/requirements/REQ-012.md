## Statement

Refreshing the same available Display video card shall retain its player and playback position. Changing the card, media path or availability shall retire the previous player.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The same video continues playing across repeated refreshes, including card revisions.
- [x] Leaving the video retires and pauses its player.

## Verification

The native `--test-video-refresh` mode exercised a synthetic video through repeated refreshes. Playback remained at rate 1 after 60 and 120 seconds (59.50 and 119.53 seconds respectively), the player identity was unchanged, and leaving the card paused and retired it; all checks passed.
