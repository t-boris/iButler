## Requirement

Include the newer local Display and written conversation history work. Persist it across restarts, pause and elapsed days until explicit Clear, without automatic count pruning. DEC-009 supersedes the earlier Display owner gate: history, selection, local actions and Clear do not consult biometric or observation permissions. An interrupted written turn must not restart automatically. Mail bodies and drafts remain in the mail store rather than being duplicated in UI history.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Real database reopen restores history, replacements and current selection.
- [x] Loss of verification or observation permission does not hide Display history or refuse selection/local controls.
- [x] Owner Clear removes the relevant history; access loss or restart does not delete it.
- [x] Interrupted turns are recorded as interrupted without automatic replay; mail bodies/drafts are not duplicated.

Decisions: DEC-008, DEC-009. The Q-007 gate choice is superseded by the later explicit owner request.
