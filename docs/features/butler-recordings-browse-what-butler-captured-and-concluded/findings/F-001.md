## Finding

The Idea leaves full meeting originals out of scope pending an owner decision, while the accepted requirements require local retention of marked meetings' microphone and system audio for 30 days. The scope statement needs to reflect the settled audio policy so implementers can distinguish it from the unresolved scope of other originals.

> Out of scope until the owner decides: keeping full meeting originals (privacy and consent decisions)

## Possible interpretations

- All meeting originals, including audio, remain out of scope.
- Marked-meeting audio is in scope, while automatic retention of other meeting originals remains out of scope.

## Resolution

Включить сохранение аудио и видео отмеченных встреч на 30 дней. — see DEC-006.

## Implementation

Resolution applied in the Recordings storage/browser and Display access checks. `tests/test_recordings.py` and `tests/test_display.py` verify fixed retention, owner-only visibility and independent media-file actions. Exact-command release results are recorded in implementation/verification.md and the linked local release report.
