## Finding

The specification makes stored recordings owner-only under observation visibility rules but also includes generated images and the in-memory display history. Project documentation describes a separate display policy under which valid cards remain visible without a private-session gate. It is unclear whether generated images already shown on the Display must acquire the stricter Recordings visibility rule, or whether the rule applies only inside Recordings.

> Everything is local only and follows the existing visibility rules.

## Possible interpretations

- Generated images and display-history material are owner-only everywhere they are shown.
- Recordings applies owner-only visibility while the existing Display visibility policy remains unchanged.

## Resolution

Показывать сгенерированные изображения и историю Display только проверенному владельцу и в «Записях», и на Display. — see DEC-004.
