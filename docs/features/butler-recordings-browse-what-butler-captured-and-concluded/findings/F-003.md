## Finding

The owner can preserve and delete timeline items, but an item can contain independently retained facts, transcripts, key frames and originals. The specification does not establish whether preservation overrides the fixed 30-day expiry or what deletion removes. These choices materially change retention and the meaning of the controls.

> The owner can preserve and delete available timeline items using the existing controls.

## Possible interpretations

- Preserve extends the lifetime of all associated content and delete removes all of it.
- Preserve and delete apply only to selected media, while transcripts and other associated records follow their own retention policies.
- Preserve and delete operate on the entire item, but fixed transcript and meeting-audio expiry still applies.

## Resolution

Сохранение и удаление применяются к выбранному медиафайлу; факты, транскрипты и другие связанные записи следуют собственным правилам хранения. — see DEC-005.

## Implementation

Resolution applied in the Recordings storage/browser and Display access checks. `tests/test_recordings.py` and `tests/test_display.py` verify fixed retention, owner-only visibility and independent media-file actions. Exact-command release results are recorded in implementation/verification.md and the linked local release report.
