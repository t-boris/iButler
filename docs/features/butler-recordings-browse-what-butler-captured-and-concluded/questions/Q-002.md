## Question

Should the original audio and video of a marked meeting be kept after analysis, and for how long?

## Why it matters

Originals are deleted right after analysis, and in any case within 24 h. On 2026-10-25 the owner chose to keep only the conclusions for that meeting. A general rule decides whether the Recordings section can ever play back a meeting. It also sets the disk budget: camera clips are about 97 % of raw volume, roughly 280 MB per hour.

## Options

- **A**: Never keep originals automatically; the owner preserves single items by hand (REQ-004)
- **B**: Keep the audio (mic + system) of marked meetings for 30 days, no camera video
- **C**: Keep audio and video of marked meetings until the owner deletes them

## Recommended

B

## Answer

B. Keep the audio (mic + system) of marked meetings for 30 days, no camera video
