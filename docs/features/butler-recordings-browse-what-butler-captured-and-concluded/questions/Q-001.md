## Question

Should Butler keep a text transcript of meetings and conversations?

## Why it matters

Today no transcript is ever written to disk, and the analysis must not quote speech. A meeting therefore leaves only facts such as "activity: video call" and "topic: …", not what was said. The owner expects to "record all conversations and analyse them later", which this cannot deliver. Keeping a transcript changes the privacy model of the vision feature, which says raw speech stays transient. It also records other people, and Illinois requires the consent of all parties.

## Options

- **A**: No transcripts (as today): only facts and key frames
- **B**: Transcripts only for meetings the owner marks (a «Записывать встречу» toggle shown while a call is detected), stored locally with a retention the owner sets, never sent to the cloud
- **C**: Transcripts of all of the owner's speech and of every detected call, local, with a fixed retention

## Recommended

B

## Answer

C. Transcripts of all of the owner's speech and of every detected call, local, with a fixed retention
