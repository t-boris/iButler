## Question

Should Butler be able to search and show recordings by voice ("покажи, что было на встрече в 8 вечера")?

## Why it matters

Today the voice brain is the cloud agent. Answering by voice means either sending facts to the cloud, or adding a local-only command that opens the Recordings section at a time and sends nothing to the cloud. REQ-005 currently forbids the cloud path.

## Options

- **A**: Panel only, no voice access
- **B**: A local voice command opens the Recordings section at the named time; nothing goes to the cloud
- **C**: The agent may read the owner's own facts (not other people's) to answer questions about them

## Recommended

B

## Answer

B. A local voice command opens the Recordings section at the named time; nothing goes to the cloud
